N.C. Gen. Stat. § 15A-1415(f)

NORTH CAROLINA GENERAL STATUTE ENTRY

Last Revised • July 9, 2026

This Source Verification Page documents North Carolina General Statute § 15A-1415(f), cited in Claim I of the Supplemental Motion. This statute establishes discovery rights available to post-conviction counsel in capital cases and is cited in support of the Defendant's claim regarding access to investigative, prosecutorial, and law enforcement records relevant to his post-conviction proceedings.

Statutory Purpose

North Carolina General Statute § 15A-1415(f) governs post-conviction discovery in capital cases and specifies the categories of investigative and prosecutorial records that may be made available to post-conviction counsel, subject to the limitations established by law.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May's reliance upon N.C. Gen. Stat. § 15A-1415(f) for the statutory requirement that post-conviction counsel in capital cases be provided access to the complete files of all law enforcement agencies and prosecutorial offices involved in the investigation and prosecution of the defendant, subject only to the limited exceptions established by statute. May cites this statutory authority in support of his allegations that he was entitled to discovery of investigative and prosecutorial files, including materials relating to the handling, preservation, and forensic testing of physical evidence in his case.
