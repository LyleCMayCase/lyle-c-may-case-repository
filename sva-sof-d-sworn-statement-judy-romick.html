Sworn Statement of APD Detective Judy Romick

GOVERNMENT RECORD ENTRY

Last Revised • July 7, 2026

This Source Verification Page documents the sworn statement of Asheville Police Department Detective Judy Romick prepared during the July 1997 investigation of the murders for which Lyle C. May was convicted. The statement documents Detective Romick's involvement in the investigation and describes the preparation of the Defendant's confession. Statement of Facts D relies upon this document to identify investigative personnel involved in the interrogation process, document Detective Forrest Weaver's participation in the preparation of the confession, and establish the involvement of specific Asheville Police Department investigators in the case.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May alleges that Asheville Police Department Detective Forrest Weaver participated in the investigation and interrogation that resulted in the Defendant's confession. Detective Judy Romick's sworn statement is cited to establish Weaver's presence during the preparation of the confession and his role as a witness to the document. May relies upon this source to document Weaver's direct involvement in the investigation, identify law-enforcement personnel who participated in the interrogation process, and connect personnel involved in the Walter Bowman investigation with personnel who later participated in the investigation and prosecution of the July 1997 Asheville murder case.
