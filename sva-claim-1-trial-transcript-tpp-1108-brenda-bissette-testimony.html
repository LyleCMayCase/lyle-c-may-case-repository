Cited to Support

Lyle C. May cites this portion of the trial transcript to establish that SBI Agent Brenda Bissette served as the State's principal forensic DNA and serology witness at trial. According to the cited testimony, Bissette presented scientific evidence collected during the investigation and served as the primary witness through whom the prosecution introduced significant portions of its forensic evidence to the jury.

May relies upon this testimony because Bissette's credibility and forensic work later became the subject of extensive scrutiny during the North Carolina State Bureau of Investigation forensic audit conducted by former FBI Assistant Director Chris Swecker and District Attorney Thomas Wolf. That statewide review identified numerous deficiencies in the handling and reporting of forensic evidence within the SBI laboratory and specifically examined multiple cases involving Bissette's work. Because Bissette served as the State's principal forensic witness in May's trial, he argues that her testimony and forensic conclusions are of particular significance when evaluating the reliability of the scientific evidence presented to the jury and the issues raised in Claim I.
