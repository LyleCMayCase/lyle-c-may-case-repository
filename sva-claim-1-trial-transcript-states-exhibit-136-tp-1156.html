State v. May Trial Transcript

TRIAL TRANSCRIPT ENTRY

Last Revised • July 8, 2026

This Source Verification Page documents portions of the State v. May trial transcript cited in Claim I of the Supplemental Motion. The cited testimony concerns Officer Darren Moore's identification of State's Exhibit 136 and his testimony regarding Lyle C. May's statements following his arrest and transport to the Asheville Police Department. The testimony is cited to document May's repeated denial of involvement in the reported assault during the initial stages of police custody.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May cites the testimony of Asheville Police Department Officer Darren Moore concerning State's Exhibit 136 to support his allegations regarding his statements immediately following his arrest. According to the cited testimony, Officer Moore identified State's Exhibit 136 and testified that May remained calm, cooperative, denied any knowledge of the incident under investigation, and stated that he had nothing to do with the reported assault while being transported to the Asheville Police Department. May relies upon this testimony to support his argument that he consistently denied involvement during the earliest stages of police custody, before the interrogation that forms the basis of Claim I.
