Trial Transcript Entry

This Source Verification Page documents portions of the State v. May trial transcript cited in Statement of Facts D of the Supplemental Motion. The cited testimony concerns Asheville Police Department Detective Judy Romick's preparation of Lyle C. May's written confession, Detective Forrest Weaver's participation in the investigation, and testimony regarding Officer Judy Hensley's observations of May prior to interrogation. Statement of Facts D relies upon these transcript passages to document how testimony concerning Hensley's observations was introduced through Detective Romick and to identify law-enforcement personnel involved in the interrogation and confession process.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May alleges that Officer Judy Hensley, a witness who personally observed him for several hours prior to interrogation, was not called to testify at either the pretrial suppression hearing or trial. The cited transcript passages are relied upon to establish that Detective Judy Romick testified regarding observations attributed to Officer Hensley despite Hensley not being presented as a witness. May relies upon these transcript passages to support his claim that the defense was denied the opportunity to directly cross-examine Officer Hensley regarding his condition, demeanor, and conduct prior to interrogation and that testimony concerning Officer Hensley's observations was instead introduced through Detective Judy Romick. The cited testimony is further relied upon to document Detective Forrest Weaver's involvement in the investigation and the preparation of May's confession.
