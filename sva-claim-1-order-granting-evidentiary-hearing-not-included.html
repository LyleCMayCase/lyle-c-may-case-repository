Cited to Support

Lyle C. May's reliance upon the May 5, 2009 Order Granting Evidentiary Hearing for the court's determination that substantial questions existed regarding his mental condition, his capacity to understand and waive his constitutional rights, and trial counsel's investigation and presentation of mental-health evidence. May cites the Order in support of his allegations that the Buncombe County Superior Court determined these issues warranted further factual development through an evidentiary hearing.

May further relies upon the Order in support of his allegations concerning the voluntariness and reliability of his statements to law enforcement and trial counsel's handling of mental-health evidence. Although the Order itself was not available for independent review during preparation of this Source Verification Archive, it is cited in the Supplemental Motion as evidence that the court previously determined these issues warranted further factual development through an evidentiary hearing.
