This Source Verification Page documents the sworn statement of Asheville Police Department Officer Judy A. Hensley cited in Claim I of the Supplemental Motion. The statement records Officer Hensley's observations of Lyle C. May while he remained in police custody prior to interrogation. Claim I cites this source to document his physical condition, level of fatigue, and circumstances of detention before questioning began.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May cites the sworn statement of Asheville Police Department Officer Judy A. Hensley to support his allegations regarding the circumstances of his detention before interrogation. Officer Hensley documented observing May repeatedly attempting to sleep while he remained in police custody as detectives interviewed Darrell Godfrey.

May relies upon these observations to support his argument that Asheville Police Department officers were aware of his fatigue and physical condition before questioning began. The statement is cited as evidence that his condition immediately preceding the interrogation should be considered when evaluating the voluntariness of his subsequent statements under the totality-of-the-circumstances standard.
