Murder Witness Claims Intimidation

ARTICLE SOURCE ENTRY

Last Revised • July 7, 2026

This Source Verification Page documents an Associated Press article published through StarNews Online concerning allegations made by witness Lynette Smith during proceedings related to the murder of Mary Elizabeth Judd in Buncombe County, North Carolina. The article discusses claims of witness intimidation, challenges to investigative practices, and concerns regarding the reliability of testimony used in a major criminal prosecution. Statement of Facts D relies upon this reporting as independent documentation of allegations involving former Buncombe County Sheriff Bobby Medford and questions surrounding the methods used to obtain witness statements during criminal investigations.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May alleges that John Collins and David Hammack were jailed in connection with the rape and murder of Mary Elizabeth Judd based largely upon statements attributed to a single eyewitness, Lynette Smith. The article is cited to document Smith's later claim that Buncombe County Sheriff Bobby Medford threatened or intimidated her into making statements implicating Collins and Hammack, as well as reporting that defense attorneys challenged the reliability of the investigation and the prosecution's reliance upon her account. May relies upon this source to support his allegation that coercive investigative practices, witness intimidation, and questionable witness testimony were not isolated incidents within Buncombe County criminal investigations. He further cites this reporting to demonstrate that concerns regarding the conduct of law enforcement officials and prosecutors arose in multiple serious felony cases originating in Asheville and Buncombe County, North Carolina.
