State v. Bates

CASE ENTRY

Last Revised • July 9, 2026

This Source Verification Page documents the North Carolina Supreme Court's decision in State v. Bates, cited in Claim I of the Supplemental Motion. The opinion discusses post-conviction discovery obligations and is cited together with N.C. Gen. Stat. § 15A-1415(f) in support of Lyle C. May's claim regarding access to investigative and prosecutorial records during post-conviction proceedings.

Cited to Support

Lyle C. May's reliance upon State v. Bates, 497 S.E.2d 276 (N.C. 1998), together with N.C. Gen. Stat. § 15A-1415(f), for the principle that post-conviction counsel is entitled to access investigative and prosecutorial records maintained by agencies involved in the investigation and prosecution of a capital case. May cites Bates in support of his allegations that records maintained by the Buncombe County Sheriff's Office and the Asheville Police Department should have been disclosed during his post-conviction proceedings.
